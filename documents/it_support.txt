Company IT Support Policy

1. Password Reset
Employees who forget their passwords should use the company's approved password-reset process. If self-service recovery fails, contact the IT support team.

2. Technical Issues
Employees should report hardware, software, or network problems through the official IT support ticketing system.

3. Support Hours
The IT support team is available Monday to Friday, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

4. Security
Employees must never share their passwords. Suspicious emails and potential security incidents should be reported to the IT security team immediately.
