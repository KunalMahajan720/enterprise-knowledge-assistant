Company HR Policy

1. Annual Leave
Employees receive 20 days of annual paid leave per calendar year. Leave requests should be submitted to the reporting manager at least five working days in advance.

2. Sick Leave
Employees may take sick leave when they are unwell. A medical certificate may be requested for extended periods of absence, according to company policy.

3. Work Hours
The standard working schedule is Monday to Friday, from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, with a lunch break.

4. Remote Work
Employees must obtain manager approval before working remotely. Remote-work arrangements must follow the company's information security guidelines.
